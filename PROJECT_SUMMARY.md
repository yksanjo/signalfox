# SignalFox - Project Summary

## 🎯 **What We Built**

**SignalFox** is a modern signal intelligence platform for B2B sales teams that
tracks funding rounds, hiring, and expansion signals from companies you want to
sell to.

## 🚀 **Live Deployment**

**<https://signal-fox.vercel.app>** (will be updated after redeploy)

## 💰 **Business Model**

- **Price**: $99/month vs ZoomInfo's $1,250+/month (90% cheaper)
- **Target**: 100 customers @ $299/year = $29,900 revenue
- **Market**: B2B sales teams at startups (Seed to Series B)

## 🛠 **Tech Stack**

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Prisma, PostgreSQL
- **Integrations**: Stripe, OpenAI, Apify, Resend
- **Deployment**: Vercel + Railway

## 📊 **Features Delivered**

1. ✅ **Landing Page** - Complete marketing site

4. ✅ **Mock Data** - Demo signals for testing
5. ✅ **Pricing Page** - Three-tier pricing
