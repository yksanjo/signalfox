## 🎨 Brand Guidelines

### Colors

- Primary Blue: #0057FF
- Success Green: #10B981
- Warning Orange: #F59E0B
- Error Red: #EF4444
- Text Dark: #111827
- Text Light: #6B7280
- Background: #F9FAFB

### Fonts

- Headings: Inter Bold
- Body: Inter Regular
- Code: JetBrains Mono

### Logo Usage

- Always use with sufficient white space
- Minimum size: 24px height
- Never stretch or distort
- Use blue (#0057FF) or white on dark backgrounds

### Voice & Tone

- Professional but approachable
- Clear and concise
- Benefit-focused
- Confident but not arrogant
- Helpful and educational

### Messaging Principles

1. Focus on customer outcomes, not features
2. Use simple language (no jargon)
3. Be specific with numbers and results
4. Show, don't just tell
5. Build trust with social proof
